## Contact support

Email [support@aegisrunner.com](mailto:support@aegisrunner.com) with:

- The project name and run or scan ID.
- The approximate time and timezone.
- The action you took, what you expected, and what happened.
- Relevant error text and screenshots with secrets removed.

Do not send passwords, session cookies, CI tokens, or payment card details. If you share a public report link, verify that its evidence is appropriate to share.
