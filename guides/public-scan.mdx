The free website scan is a quick way to inspect a public target before creating a testing workflow in the dashboard.

## Submit the website

1. Open [Scan Your App Free](https://aegisrunner.com/scan).
2. Enter the URL of a website you own or are authorized to test.
3. Complete any displayed verification and confirm the testing terms.
4. Start the scan and keep the result link.
5. Review the reached pages and available findings when it finishes.

<Image src="https://aegisrunner.com/docs-assets/free-scan.png" alt="AegisRunner public website scan entry form" />

## Continue with maintained testing

A public scan is bounded and does not establish coverage of protected pages or every app workflow. Create and verify an account to work with project access, generated suites, repeat runs, and automation available on your plan.

Follow [Your first website scan](/quickstart) for the project workflow and [App access](/guides/authentication) for protected pages.

Native app testing uses a test build and the project's mobile entitlements. See [Mobile testing](/guides/mobile-testing).
