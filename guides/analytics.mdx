---
title: "Analytics and test trends"
description: "Interpret execution history and compare results across consistent testing conditions."
---

Open **Analytics** in the project to examine available run and testing trends. Use it to decide where to investigate, then open the underlying execution for evidence.

## Review the scope

Check the project, date range, selected scan, and any filters offered by the view. Counts from different suites or surfaces do not necessarily measure the same work.

Compare passed, failed, skipped, and needs-review results separately. A lower failure count can result from running fewer tests rather than fixing an application defect.

## Investigate changes

When a trend changes, compare the target environment, account role, data, browser, and suite selection. Look for a new deployment, expired access, regenerated tests, or a changed test expectation.

Open the relevant run to inspect cases and steps. Use [Failure triage](/guides/failure-triage) if multiple failures may share a cause.

## Handle intermittent results

Repeat a focused case under the same conditions to determine whether it is stable. Inspect timing, data dependencies, and selectors before changing expectations.

Keep useful cases enabled and reviewed. If a case is temporarily excluded or quarantined through an available control, record why and restore it after correcting the cause.
