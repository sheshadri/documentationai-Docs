Open **Project setup → What AegisRunner learned** to inspect the app knowledge and check history available for your project.

## Review learned facts

Inspect the scope, role, source, confidence, and observed behavior of a fact. Confirm it when it matches your app and correct or unconfirm an interpretation that is wrong.

An inferred page purpose or action is different from a guarantee you have explicitly supplied. Review facts for the role that was scanned rather than assuming every user sees the same workflow.

## Judge check quality

Review a check pattern's results and available examples. Mark a confirmed real problem or a false alarm only after examining the application evidence.

A failed run does not by itself establish whether the app or the test was wrong. These judgments help you separate useful checks from noisy expectations.

## Supply scan hints

Use the available scan-hints section to describe important paths, expected business behavior, and workflows that need particular preparation. Give concrete steps, roles, and outcomes rather than a vague instruction to test everything.

For example: `As a Customer, create a draft request. As an Admin, open the requests list and verify the same reference and requested amount.`

Save the hint and run the relevant exploration or test. Review the resulting behavior and evidence; saving a hint alone does not prove that the workflow was reached.
