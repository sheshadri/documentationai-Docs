---
title: "Issues and coverage"
description: "Read issue families, distinguish observations from confirmed defects, and inspect coverage gaps."
---

Issues describe findings from reached pages, screens, or test runs. Coverage describes what was exercised and what remains to check. Use both when assessing a release.

## Investigate a finding

Open Issues and inspect its category, severity, affected page, and available evidence. A page navigation error, failed API call, missing control behavior, and accessibility finding can require different fixes.

Check the selected scan and role. A finding on a sign-in screen may be an access problem rather than a defect in the protected page you intended to test.

## Review issue families

Use **Issue families** to examine related findings together. Compare the shared cause and affected locations before treating every member as a separate defect.

If you use an issue tracker, verify whether the integration creates an issue per failed test or another supported grouping; do not assume that the display grouping changes the external automation.

## Inspect coverage

Open Coverage for the intended scan or available combined view. Review covered capabilities and gaps by category. Prioritize essential workflows that were not reached or verified.

A passing test count alone does not establish coverage of every role, route, or device. A skipped case or an unverified saved record should remain visible in your assessment.

## Act on recommendations

Open Recommendations to inspect available suggestions. Check their evidence and relevance to your app, then fix the app or improve the testing setup as appropriate.

Repeat the affected checks after a change. See [Persistence checks](/guides/persistence-checks) and [Failure triage](/guides/failure-triage) for specific evidence workflows.
