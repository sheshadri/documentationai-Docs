---
title: "Run your first website scan"
description: "Create a project from a website URL, configure access, and inspect your first results."
---

Use a staging website you can test and a dedicated test account if sign-in is required. For native apps, follow [Mobile testing](/guides/mobile-testing).

## Sign in

[Create an AegisRunner account](https://app.aegisrunner.com/register), then open the [dashboard](https://app.aegisrunner.com/app).

The credentials you use to sign in to AegisRunner are separate from the credentials for the app you want to test.

## Choose your website

1. Select **Website** on the dashboard.
2. Enter your website URL, such as `https://staging.example.com`.
3. Select **Start testing**.
4. Check the **Website URL** in the scan dialog and continue to the scan options.

Starting a scan from the dashboard creates or resolves a project for the target. For an existing project, start the scan from that project's workspace.

## Configure access and scan behavior

1. If your app needs sign-in, add its test account in **Does your app need a login?** Saved logins can also be managed in [App access](/guides/authentication).
2. Confirm that you own the app or are authorized to test it.
3. Review the option to allow changes. Enable it only when creating, editing, and deleting test data is appropriate for this environment.
4. Under scan detail, use **Smart scan** for representative checks across repeated page types, or **Full detail** for deeper checks on each reached page.
5. Select **Start scan**.

Both scan modes stay within your plan's scan limits. Full detail can take longer; completion time depends on the app, access, and the work discovered.

## Inspect what was reached

Open **Activity** for progress. In the project, use **Overview** to inspect pages and screenshots as they arrive. Test generation can continue after page discovery finishes.

Check that protected pages appear if you supplied a login. If you only see the sign-in page, correct the login before treating the scan as coverage of your app.

## Run the generated tests

1. Open **Tests** and inspect a generated suite.
2. Check that its actions and expected outcomes match your app.
3. Start a run for the suite.
4. Open **Runs** to inspect case results and available step evidence.

See [Test suites and runs](/guides/test-suites) and [Reading results](/guides/results) for the next steps.
