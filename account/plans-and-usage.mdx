title: "Plans and usage"
description: "Find current plan limits, usage, and upgrade options."
---

Open account **Settings → Billing & plan**, then **Plan & usage** to see your subscription and current usage. Use the values shown there for your account rather than assuming every project has the same limits.

## Understand the limits

Your plan can limit the number of projects, scans, test runs, pages per scan, and access to features. Usage and feature availability are separate: having scan capacity does not grant every integration or execution option.

CI triggers require Pro or Business. Other feature gates are shown where you use the feature.

## Check reset dates

The billing screen displays when scans and test runs reset. These usage periods can differ from your subscription's payment date.

When a limit is reached, inspect the affected resource and reset date before retrying. Repeating the same request does not increase the quota.

## Change capacity

Review the current plans in the billing screen or on the [pricing page](https://aegisrunner.com/pricing). The billing owner can choose an available plan and payment interval.

Current prices, allowances, and any available offers are shown at checkout. See [Billing and invoices](/account/billing) for subscription changes.
